What is this?

      

        

          
The model

          
This is flyvis, the model from
            Lappalainen et al., Connectome-constrained networks predict neural activity across the fly visual system (Nature, 2024).
            Its wiring comes from electron-microscopy reconstructions of the fruit fly's optic lobe. Training only tuned
            one strength per connection type, plus each cell type's resting potential and time constant, so that the network could
            report visual motion. The network running here is model flow/0000/000 from the published ensemble.

        

        

          
The simulation

          
Each neuron is a leaky, non-spiking unit and the synapses are graded, as in flyvis.
            The email is drawn onto a strip that scrolls across the eye, sampled by the same box filter flyvis uses,
            and integrated in steps of 20 ms. This JavaScript port is tested against flyvis itself and matches it to within about
            10-6.

        

        

          
What it can't do

          
This is only the visual system: the part that turns light into motion signals. There is no memory,
            no language and no decision-making here. The fly's reply tells you how its neurons responded, not what the email said.
